Voice & language

You choose a voice when you create an agent. Agents you create speak with Edge TTS, which is synthetic. The clips on the home page are recordings of real people.

Speech to text is Whisper on Groq, which copes with many accents. The quickest check is your own: talk to Maya in your browser and read the transcript.

English. Every voice we offer is an English voice. Whisper can transcribe other languages, but the agent answers with an English voice.
