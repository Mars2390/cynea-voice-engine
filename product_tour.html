Step 1 of 7 · Welcome

This is your Cynea Agent Manager

A single workspace to build, configure, monitor and launch human-like AI voice agents for your business — wherever your customers are in the world.

Take the 60-second tour, or jump straight into the dashboard whenever you like.

Create and manage voice agents

Spin up an agent in minutes. Give each one a persona, pick from natural, lifelike voices, and serve customers in their own language across global markets.

Every agent lives in one place — clone, pause, or refine any of them at any time.

Tune persona, voice and rules

Shape exactly how your agent sounds and behaves. Set tone and personality, choose a voice, connect a knowledge base, and define escalation rules for when a human should step in.

Changes apply instantly — test, adjust, and re-test until it feels right.

Attach a number in minutes

Connect a phone number through Twilio and major carriers worldwide, or bring your own. Inbound and outbound calls route to your agent automatically.

No telecom expertise required — provision, link, and you're taking calls.

Watch calls as they happen

The live monitor shows active calls in real time — live transcripts, agent status, and call duration as the conversation unfolds.

Step in, take over, or simply observe how your agents handle every call.

Measure containment, sentiment & cost

Track the metrics that matter: how many calls your agents resolve on their own, customer sentiment, average handling time, and cost per call.

Every report is exportable, so insights flow straight into your own tools.

Launch to the world

You're ready. Flip your agent live and it starts taking calls — answering customers around the clock, in every market you serve.

Welcome aboard. Your dashboard is waiting.
