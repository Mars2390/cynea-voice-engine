How it works

From quiet repo to live phone number in three steps.

01

Upload content

Point Cynea at your scripts, PDFs, knowledge base or website. The agent learns your business, not the other way around.

02

Configure agent

Pick a persona, voice, escalation rules and pricing card. Validate the agent in the dashboard before going live.

03

Deploy to phone

Get an Africa's Talking or Twilio number routed to the agent in minutes. Pay per minute used; cancel anytime.

Built for African business

Eight choices we made differently from US-first voice AI platforms.

African accents & languages

English variants for Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa — plus mixed Swahili tokens.

Human-like conversations

Sequence-id barge-in, grace periods, backchannels. No IVR feel.

Free voice processing

Local Whisper STT + free Edge TTS by default. You only pay LLM tokens and telephony.

Operations dashboard included

Per-call sentiment, containment, cost. Export PDF + CSV. Print-friendly.

Zero infrastructure cost

Runs on a single 8 GB box. No Kubernetes, no Redis cluster required.

African phone numbers

Africa's Talking integration for Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda.

Sentiment & analytics

Per-call sentiment scoring, containment trends, agent-vs-agent comparison.

24 / 7 availability

Engine handles concurrent calls; metrics tracker keeps the audit trail.

Live call history with containment, sentiment and cost per call.
