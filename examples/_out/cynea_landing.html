How it works

From quiet repo to live phone number

Three steps. Same engine, any client.

01

Upload

Point Cynea at your scripts, PDFs, knowledge base or website. The agent learns your business, not the other way around.

02

Configure

Pick a persona, voice, language and escalation rules. Validate the agent in the dashboard before going live.

03

Deploy

Get an Africa's Talking or Twilio number routed to the agent in minutes. Pay per minute used; cancel anytime.

Features

Eight choices we made differently

Where US-first voice AI platforms get Africa wrong, and where we get it right.

African Voices

Voices for Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, South Africa — plus mixed Swahili tokens.

Human-like Speech

Sequence-id barge-in, grace periods, backchannels. No IVR feel.

Free STT/TTS

Local Whisper + free Edge TTS by default. You only pay LLM tokens and telephony.

Dashboard Included

Per-call sentiment, containment, cost. Export PDF + CSV. Print-friendly.

Sentiment Analytics

Per-call sentiment scoring, containment trends, agent-vs-agent comparison.

24 / 7 Operation

Engine handles concurrent calls; metrics tracker keeps the audit trail.
