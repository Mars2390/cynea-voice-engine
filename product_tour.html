Step 1 of 7 · Welcome

This is your Cynea console

One place to create voice agents, edit what they are told to do, and see their calls.

Take the tour, or jump straight into the dashboard whenever you like.

Create an agent

Give it a persona and choose a voice. Agents you create speak with Edge TTS.

Every agent you create is listed in the console.

Edit its prompt

The prompt tells an agent how to behave and what it knows. Change it whenever you need to.

Every change is kept in version history.

Test it in your browser

Start a test call and talk to your agent with your microphone.

Browser calls work today. Phone calls are not connected yet.

Check the call log

The call log lists your calls in one place.

A recording appears when one was uploaded.

Connect your systems

Create API keys, and receive signed outbound webhooks in your own systems.

You can also export your account data or delete it.

That is the tour

Open the dashboard to see your agents, or create your first one.

Your dashboard is waiting.
