Getting started

From your dashboard, start a new agent and follow the guided setup: give it a name and persona, add your content, pick a voice, then connect a phone number. Most teams have a working agent in a few minutes.

Agents handle conversational tasks such as answering common questions, booking appointments, qualifying leads, taking simple requests, and routing callers. The more relevant content you upload, the better it represents your business.

Voice & language

Pricing & billing

Pricing is usage-based, so you pay for what you use. For current rates and plan options that fit your call volume, talk to our team and we will put together pricing for your needs.

You can try Cynea before committing. Book a session with our team and we will set you up so you can build an agent and hear it in action.

Billing is based on your usage over each period. You can review activity in the dashboard, and our team can help you set up the plan and limits that work for your account.
