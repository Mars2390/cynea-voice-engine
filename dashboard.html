Webhooks

We POST JSON to your URL when a call is logged. One attempt per event, with no retries. Every delivery is signed:

Cynea-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hmac_sha256(secret, "t.body")>

Copy this signing secret now. You won't see it again.
