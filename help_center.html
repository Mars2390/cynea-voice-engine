Getting started

How do I create a voice agent?+
Click "Create New Agent" from your dashboard. Follow the 6-step wizard: name your agent, pick a persona, upload your content, choose a voice, select a phone number, and deploy. It takes about five minutes.
What can my agent handle?+
Agents can book appointments, answer FAQs, take orders, handle complaints, check account balances, and more. Upload your scripts and the AI learns your business processes.
Do I need technical skills?+
No. The agent manager is designed for operations teams. Upload your existing scripts and documents — our AI does the rest. No coding, no DevOps.

Voice & language

What voices are available?+
We offer 30+ natural voices across English (British, American, African-accented), French, and Swahili. You can preview any voice before deploying.
Can the agent handle accents?+
Yes. Our speech recognition is trained on diverse African accents including Ghanaian, Kenyan, Nigerian, and South African English.
What languages do you support?+
Currently English with African accents. Swahili and French coming Q4 2026. Code-switching (mixing languages mid-sentence) works for common English-Swahili patterns.

Pricing & billing

How much does it cost?+
You pay per minute of call time. Starter plan: $0.04/min. Business: $0.03/min. Enterprise: custom pricing. No setup fees, no monthly minimum.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes. First 500 minutes are free. No credit card required.
How does billing work?+
Monthly invoice based on usage. You can set spending limits and get alerts when you approach them.

Technical

How do I connect my phone number?+
We integrate with Africa's Talking, Twilio, and major African carriers. Our team helps you set up in under 30 minutes.
Can the agent transfer to a human?+
Yes. Warm transfers with full conversation summary. You define escalation triggers — angry customer, complex issue, VIP caller.
Is my data secure?+
All call data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We comply with African data protection regulations. Call recordings can be stored on your own infrastructure.
What happens if the internet goes down?+
Agents can fall back to a basic IVR mode — critical for areas with intermittent connectivity. Full offline mode coming Q1 2027.
